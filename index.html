Project Overview

This project represents a fully custom server-side modification for Left 4 Dead 2, written entirely in Squirrel (VScript). It drastically expands the vanilla cooperative experience by introducing a dynamic economy, a persistent personal upgrade system, real-time dynamic damage scaling, and a fully functional hierarchical admin command system.

The mod captures thousands of game events per match (damage, kills, revives, items) and processes them through custom logic gates without causing server lag or engine crashes, ensuring high-performance gameplay.

Core Systems & Features

🛒 Dynamic Economy (RenShop)

A fully integrated chat-based point shop allowing players to purchase weapons, items, and upgrades dynamically.

  • Action-Based Earnings: Players earn points for killing Special Infected, healing teammates, and stopping Hunters/Smokers.
  • Coupon System: Rare weapon coupons (e.g., AR15, Universal, Heavy) granted for hitting milestone kill counts.
  • Jackpot Roll: Built-in RNG that grants lucky players massive point boosts, Guaranteed Life Steal, or Universal Coupons.

⚙️ Progressive Upgrade System

Players can invest their earned points into permanent character buffs that reset upon death.

  • Max HP (up hp): Dynamically increases maximum survivor health beyond the standard 100 HP limit.
  • Damage Boost (up dmg): Applies a flat percentage multiplier to all outgoing gun and melee damage.
  • Defense (up def): Reduces incoming damage strictly from zombies (ignores friendly fire to maintain balance).
  • Dynamic Pricing: Upgrade costs scale exponentially based on the current level.

🛡️ Advanced Admin Hierarchy

A secure, two-tiered admin system bound by Steam ID networks to prevent spoofing and abuse.

  • Master Admin: Unrestricted access. Can reset upgrades (sys reset), wipe points (sys rpoint), grant infinite points (sys apoint 9999), and grant admin privileges to others.
  • Regular Admin: Capable of managing the players wallet but restricted by hard caps (e.g., max 1000 points per transaction, limited coupons).
  • Dynamic Targeting: Commands target specific character models (e.g., coach, producer) or all players simultaneously.

🩸 Custom Combat Mechanics

Vanilla engine mechanics completely overhauled using the AllowTakeDamage hook.

  • Smart Friendly Fire: Punishes reckless players. Hitting teammates 5 times activates a reflection multiplier. The script tracks individual hits via timestamps to avoid multi-pellet shotgun bugs.
  • Incap AoE Explosions: When overwhelmed, incapacitated players trigger a massive defensive blast, killing nearby infected without harming teammates.
  • Melee Life Steal: Killing infected with specific weapons (like the Knife) grants temporary/permanent health back to the attacker.

⚡ Self Auto-Revive

A custom fail-safe that automatically resurrects a dead survivor after a 3-second delay, provided they have earned a Revive Coupon.

  • The Pity System: If a player dies 3 times, the system takes pity on them and automatically grants a revive coupon so they don't become a burden to the team.
  • Milestone Rewards: Highly skilled players can earn these coupons by reaching the highest reward tiers (e.g., killing 60 Special Infected, slaying 4 Bosses, or healing 15 teammates).
  • Automated Execution: Seamlessly hooks into the player death event, broadcasting a countdown before triggering a remote entity fire to apply a defibrillator-style revival.

Technical Highlights & Engine Hacks

Short-Circuit String Parsing

Optimized chat command handler using split(cmd, " ") and index slicing. Safely extracts command arrays while utilizing engine short-circuits (e.g., args.len() >= 4) to prevent out-of-bounds crashes during rapid chat spam.

NetProp Manipulation

Directly intercepts and manipulates entity properties (e.g., m_strMapSetScriptName for custom melee weapons and m_bIsOnThirdStrike for black-and-white state detection) safely wrapped in validation checks.

Damage Table Overrides

Hooks directly into the engine's AllowTakeDamage(damageTable). Dynamically edits the DamageDone float before the server processes it. Accurately filters out worldspawn damage, fall damage, and Special Infected Ghost Mode states (!victim.IsGhost()) to prevent silent script crashes.

Project Overview

This module completely overhauls the vanilla Left 4 Dead 2 weapon balancing, creating distinct tiers and roles for every firearm in the game. It emphasizes positioning, movement management, and situational awareness.

Core Systems & Features

📈 Rebalanced Firearm Stats

Complete adjustment of base damage, clip sizes, and reserve ammunition. Weapons are now highly specialized (e.g., smaller clips but devastating damage per round).

🏃 Variable Player Speed

Movement speed is dynamically calculated based on the equipped weapon's weight. SMGs grant maximum mobility (no penalty), while heavy sniper rifles like the AS50 severely restrict movement (-50% speed).

💥 Oblivion Grenade Launcher

A custom-scripted dynamic damage system for the Grenade Launcher. Damage scales based on proximity to the blast center and the specific infected class caught in the explosion.

🌟 "Legendary" Tier Weapons

Formerly the international CS:S weapons. These firearms now possess god-tier stats but have an astronomically low natural spawn rate (0.0001%), acting as legendary drops unless manually placed by map creators.

🛡️ Penetrable Riot Armor

Riot Infected (Police Zombies) are no longer completely invincible from the front! Every weapon now possesses a unique Riot Shield Penetration stat, determining exactly how much damage bleeds through their armor.

Legendary Tier Weapons

Knife

The Knife is the ultimate mobility and survival melee tool. Equipping it grants a passive 25% boost to movement speed, allowing for rapid repositioning. It is engineered with a massive 40-hitray arc, making 360-degree crowd control effortless when surrounded. Additionally, every strike has a 2% chance to trigger life steal, restoring 5 health points per proc to keep you in the fight.

MP5SD

The MP5SD reigns as the most accurate weapon within the SMG category. It boasts exceptional damage and a blistering rate of fire, balanced only by a slower reload time. Unique to this legendary tier, the MP5SD features a fully functional Aim Down Sights (ADS) system, allowing players to utilize its red dot scope for pinpoint precision during mid-range engagements.

AR15

The AR15 is a legendary assault platform that stands as the second most accurate weapon in the entire arsenal. To capitalize on this accuracy, it features a custom Aim Down Sights (ADS) system equipped with a duplex sniper scope, providing an extreme zoom factor that actually surpasses standard sniper rifles. It delivers immense damage capable of immediately staggering sitting Witches and operates with unlimited effective range and zero damage fall-off. Its high-velocity rounds can penetrate up to 4 Common Infected (striking 5 targets total) in a single trajectory, though players must carefully manage its notably slow reload speed.

Cheytac M200 Intervention

The CheyTac M200 Intervention is the undisputed king of precision, holding the title of the most accurate weapon in the game. It possesses unlimited effective range, infinite penetration layers, and absolute zero damage fall-off. Its catastrophic damage is hard-coded to instantly execute a charging Charger on any difficulty (including Realism) regardless of where the shot lands. To balance this god-tier power, it imposes a 15% movement speed penalty and features a lengthy reload animation.

AS50

The AS50 is a devastating anti-materiel sniper rifle with a raw damage output second only to the Carl Gustav M4. It is hard-coded to instantly obliterate charging Chargers and Witches on any difficulty or hit location, and guarantees a 100% stagger rate against Tanks. Firing this behemoth produces a physical knock-back effect on the user and imposes an astronomical 50% movement speed penalty while equipped.

Due to its sheer, game-breaking power, it occupies a dedicated weapon slot (Slot 7, accessed by pressing '8'), suffers from a slow reload time, and its ammunition cannot be refilled from standard ammo piles.

Oblivion Grenade Launcher

Mechanics & Accuracy

The Oblivion Grenade Launcher (a modified Carl Gustav M4) is a custom VScript weapon that replaces standard grenade blasts with a devastating atomic payload. It utilizes a dynamic, proximity-based shockwave system rather than standard splash damage. Because of its immense weight and explosive power, its hip-fire accuracy is heavily penalized while moving. Players must stop moving and aim carefully before shooting the Carl Gustav M4. Infected caught in the shockwave aren't just damaged—they are staggered and launched into the air, while the survivors will get knockback effect. Due to its specialized nature, it occupies a dedicated weapon slot (Slot 7), meaning players must press '8' to equip it, just like the AS50.

Annihilating Tanks & Special Infected

The weapon boasts a monstrous dynamic damage output that scales massively depending on the target's distance from the epicenter. While the base configured explosion damage deals heavy area-of-effect damage, the VScript forces a hardcoded core shockwave against the Infected. Damage multipliers skyrocket the closer a target is to the blast center:

  • Epicenter (95% - 100% Proximity): A direct hit acts as an executioner. It triggers a massive 200x multiplier stacked with an 8x secondary blast, dealing a staggering 41,600 total damage. This instantly obliterates Tanks and Witches in a single shot.
  • Inner Blast Zone (92.5% - 94.9%): The shockwave retains an 8x multiplier, instantly dealing 1,600 damage to nearby tanks.
  • Mid-Blast Zone (90.0% - 92.4%): Applies a 6x multiplier, yielding 1,200 damage to nearby tanks.
  • Outer-Mid Zone (87.5% - 89.9%): Applies a 4x multiplier, dealing 800 damage to nearby tanks.
  • Blast Perimeter (85.0% - 87.4%): Applies a 3x multiplier, dealing 600 damage to nearby tanks.
  • Fringe Shockwave (< 85% Proximity): Targets caught at the very edge of the explosion take a standard 200 flat damage.
  • Any Special Infected Will took fixed 3500 damage on >= 85% perimeter

Self and Friendly Fire Damage

Friendly fire is strictly governed by an auto-generated config file (ren/oblivion.txt). When enabled, survivor damage scales based on the server difficulty. However, the epicenter is universally fatal: if a teammate (including yourself) is caught at 90%+ proximity to the blast, the shockwave bypasses all health buffers, dealing over 10,000 damage and forcing an instant death. To prevent spam and griefing, standard survivors are restricted to a slow 0.1x firing rate.

Project Overview

The Game Adjustment module encompasses comprehensive changes to the core mechanics of Left 4 Dead 2. It introduces a strict behavioral penalty system to discourage griefing, overhauls baseline gameplay rules for a more dynamic experience, and significantly buffs survivor consumables to balance out the server's increased difficulty.

Core Systems & Features

⚠️ Penalty System

A strict behavioral module designed to discourage reckless gameplay and poor trigger discipline. Features escalating friendly-fire reflected damage, economy credit deductions, and lethal self-explosions for chronic offenders.

🕹️ Game Play Adjustment

Core mechanic tweaks that change how players interact with the world. Features include infinite shoving capabilities (including shoving other survivors), 5 seconds of 100% damage immunity after being revived, noise-based horde attraction from gunshots, and RNG item drops from Special Infected and Bosses.

💊 Consumable Adjustment

Significant buffs and changes to support items to aid survival. Adrenaline grants damage reduction and lasts longer, pills provide a larger health buffer, medkits heal more efficiently and faster, and Bile Jars now act as a double-edged sword that can affect survivors.

Penalty System Details

🪞 Reflected Damage

A punishing mechanic that mirrors a percentage of friendly fire damage back to the careless attacker.

  • Base Multiplier: Starts at 20% reflected damage.
  • Escalation Phase: For every 5 friendly fire incidents, the multiplier increases by an additional 20%.
  • Infinite Scaling: There is no cap! Chronic friendly-fire offenders can instantly kill themselves if they shoot a teammate.
  • Reset Condition: The multiplier strictly resets upon map transition.

💸 Economy Credit Loss

Reckless aim directly impacts the player's wallet, bleeding their dynamic shop economy points.

  • Credit Deduction: Players have a chance to lose between 25 and 50 credits when damaging a teammate.
  • Dynamic Probability: The base chance starts at 0% and increases by 2% for every instance of friendly fire, up to 100%.
  • Reset Condition: Resets to 0% after a deduction occurs.

💣 Self-Explode on Incap

The ultimate punishment for chronic friendly fire, turning an incapacitated player into a lethal hazard.

  • Instant Execution: Upon being incapacitated, the player has a chance to instantly detonate.
  • Devastating AoE: Deals a fixed 600 damage within a 10-meter radius to everything, including teammates.
  • Dynamic Probability: Starts at 0% and increases by 2% for every friendly fire incident.

Game Play Adjustment Details

🏃 Advanced Shoving

Fatigue from shoving has been entirely removed, allowing for infinite shoving. Additionally, the physics engine has been tweaked so you can now shove other survivors, allowing you to physically push teammates out of a Charger's path or unstuck them.

🛡️ Self-Auto Revive and Post-Revival Immunity

If you fall in battle, you will automatically get back on your feet if you possess a self-revive coupon, which can be earned through reward milestones (see the Shop and Reward System section).

Additionally, to prevent immediate "death-looping" when you self-revive or a teammate gets you up in a bad spot, players are granted exactly 5 seconds of 100% absolute damage immunity immediately after being revived.

🔊 Sound Aggro Mechanics

Stealth matters. Unsuppressed gunshots are now actively registered by the AI director. Firing loud weapons accumulates noise points across the entire team. Each shot adds to a global counter based on the weapon's caliber, and once the team accumulates exactly 60,000 points, it triggers a massive panic event horde (equivalent to shooting a car alarm). Upon triggering the horde, the global point counter resets back to zero.

🎁 RPG Loot Drops

Risk yields reward. Successfully killing a Special Infected or a Boss (Tank/Witch) now triggers a random number generator that has a chance to drop valuable consumable items, keeping aggressive teams stocked up on supplies.

Consumable Adjustment Details

💉 Adrenaline Shot

Adrenaline has been upgraded from a simple speed boost to a full combat stim.

  • Damage Reduction: Grants a flat 25% reduction to all incoming damage while active.
  • Extended Duration: The effect duration has been increased by 50%, lasting a total of 22.5 seconds.

💊 Pain Pills

Pills have been chemically enhanced to provide a much wider safety net during desperate situations. Ingesting pills now provides a 75 temporary health buffer, up significantly from the vanilla 50 buffer.

🚑 First Aid Kit

Medkits are now much more efficient to use in the heat of battle.

  • Faster Application: The time it takes to patch yourself or a teammate up is exactly 1 second faster.
  • Higher Yield: Restores up to 90% of your missing health pool, a solid bump up from the standard 80%.

🤢 Bile Bomb Hazards

The Bile Bomb is no longer just an Infected attractant—it is a dangerous bio-weapon. The bile splash radius can now hit survivors. If a survivor gets splashed, they suffer the exact same effects as a Boomer vomit attack (blindness and immediate horde aggro).
